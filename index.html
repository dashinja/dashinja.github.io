About the author

I graduated from Davidson College in 2009 with a degree in Spanish Literature and a decade in the entertainment industry as a theatrical master electrician, lighting designer, and stage manager. Getting to the heart of what a client wants, translating it into the technical language of the trade, and executing on that knowledge was daily work — it still is.

These days I build full-stack web apps, GraphQL APIs, and command-line tools, all collected on this hub. I believe design and functionality should engage the user and simplify decision making. Coding is like water: you have to drink it every day. I love water.
