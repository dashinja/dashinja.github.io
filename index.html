About me

Davidson College graduate (2009, Spanish Literature) turned full-stack developer. Three years of sharpening the programming axe — Ruby first, then JavaScript, then a three-month MERN bootcamp — with GraphQL as the API of choice.

Before code, I spent years in the entertainment industry as a theatrical master electrician, lighting designer and stage manager — translating a client's dream into technical language and executing on it. Getting to the heart of what a client wants is in my blood. Delivery flows from there.
